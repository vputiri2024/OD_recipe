Juicy Grilled Chicken Breasts

Home

Ingredients List

  • 4 Skinless boneless chicken breast halves
  • 1/4 cup lemon juice, plus wedges for serving
  • 1/4 cup olive oil
  • 2 teaspoons dried oregano or parsley
  • 1 teaspoon seasoning salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground black pepper
  • 1/2 teaspoon onion powder

Directions

  1. Gather the ingredients.
  2. Pound the chicken breasts until 1/2 inch thickness.
  3. Mix all ingredients except lemon wedges for at least 30 minutes or up to 12 hours.
  4. Preheat the grill.
  5. Grill chicken about 5 minutes per side until done.
  6. Transfer chicken to a cutting board and tent with aluminum foil for 5 minutes.
  7. Serve with lemon wedges.
