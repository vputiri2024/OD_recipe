Greek Salad

Home

Ingredients

  • 1 head of romaine lettuce, rinsed, dried and chopped
  • 1 cucumber sliced
  • 2 large tomatoes chopped
  • 6 ounces pitted black olives
  • 1 red onion thinly sliced
  • 1 cup crumbled feta cheese
  • 6 tbl olive oil
  • 1 lemon juiced
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • Ground black pepper to taste

Directions

  1. Combine all vegetables together into a bowl.
  2. Whisk olive oil, lemon juice, oregano and black pepper together in bowl and pour over salad.
  3. Pour dressing over salad, toss well to combine and serve.
